# Sound sources

All imported recordings below are released under CC0 (public-domain dedication).
Only audio files were imported; no scripts or executables from these packs are used.

- **Tabasco — Gunshot sounds**: https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds
  `rifle-shot.wav`: one SKS shot cropped from `sounds/sks.wav`, mixed to mono and tail-faded.
- **SpringySpringo — Gun reload sounds**: https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds
  `reload.wav`: `assaultriflereload1_0.wav`, an airsoft rifle reload recording.
- **Fantozzi, edited/submitted by qubodup — Footsteps (Grass/Sand & Stone)**:
  https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone
  `step-*.ogg`: original Sand/Stone L1/R1/L2/R2 recordings, renamed.
- **artisticdude — RPG Sound Pack**: https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack
  `creature-1.wav` through `creature-3.wav`: gutteral beast mnstr1–3, renamed.
- **Darsycho — Monster snarls**: https://opengameart.org/content/monster-snarls
  `creature-snarl.ogg`: monster-snarls-2_0, renamed.
- **Kenney — Sci-fi Sounds**: https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds
  Download mirror: https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-sounds
  `plasma`, `impact`, `explosion`, `ship-engine`, `tractor-beam`, `cell-release`,
  `fire-breath`, `acid-spit`, `wing-rush`: respectively laserLarge_000,
  impactMetal_000, explosionCrunch_000, spaceEngineLarge_000, forceField_000,
  doorOpen_000, thrusterFire_000, slime_000, spaceEngineSmall_000. Renamed Ogg files.

The game varies pitch, pans and attenuates samples according to source position.
`files.json` records sizes and SHA-256 hashes of the imported audio.
